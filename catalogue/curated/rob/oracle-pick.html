Ask Oracle — which one, and why

Pick: prompt-kit parts under our existing Oracle state. Not assistant-ui, not the shadcn chatbot template.
The orb, the head, the cue engine, the nudge budgets, the tip drafts and the per-session thread are the product and they are tested (70+ specs). What is weak is the rendering of the thread: 579 lines of hand-rolled bubbles and 740 lines of CSS, an empty body when it opens, a wrong state asset. prompt-kit is seven small shadcn-registry files (MIT, Tailwind 4, no runtime, no transport) that replace exactly that rendering layer and nothing else. assistant-ui would put its own runtime between our state machine and the DOM; the shadcn chatbot template is welded to the AI SDK transport and Next. Both are the wrong shape for a 360px co-pilot with its own brain.
