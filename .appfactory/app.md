# SISO App Factory: what it is for

## For
Shaan, and only Shaan (idea 23). The job he hires it for: to see every project he is building as its line (what the
app is for, its features, widgets, pages and components) and push each one through that line with his agents,
choosing parts by looking at them rendered, never from a list of names. Every hub he owns (Industries, UI, Code,
Agents, the Law Book and the rest) is a core inside it, so one place holds what he builds with and what he builds.

It is one of SISO OS's three spaces (Agent Base, App Factory, Agency). SISO OS's shell (the rail, the tabs, ⌘K, the
bell) comes from AGENT-BASE's module contract; the factory brings its cores and its pages.

## It is not
- **For other people** in v1: no sign-in, no per-user storage, no hosted dashboards. A read-only public copy comes once
  he uses it daily (spec §2).
- **A store of other projects' files.** It reads each project's `.appfactory/` from disk and copies nothing.
- **A shell of its own.** The rail, tabs, ⌘K and bell are SISO OS's (AGENT-BASE); the factory is a space inside them.
- **A builder that replaces agents.** Agents do steps 1-7 of the line; the factory shows the work and takes his picks.
- **Something he pays for.** Static where it's public, his laptop where it's private; $0 a month.

## Done looks like
1. He opens the factory from SISO OS and sees his projects, each with its line and real counts.
2. A project opens to its features, widgets, pages and components, each component glowing its status.
3. Every component has one standard page that renders it live from its own code.
4. For a weak component an agent shows 2-3 variations side by side; he picks one, and his pick and words are written
   back to that project's `.appfactory/`.
5. SISO UI is a set he chose by looking, installable one line per part.
6. Industries: the all-industries table searches, and an industry shows its packs.
