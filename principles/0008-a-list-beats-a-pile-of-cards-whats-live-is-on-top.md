# ADR 0008: A list beats a pile of cards; what's live is on top

Status: accepted, round 1 (7 Oct 2026)

> "I just don't like how top level to see what agents are working inside I got to click on the drop down… Also idle ones should be batched"
>
> — Shaan, 6 Oct 22:35 · 5 Oct: "you'd have the tree and maybe you hover over them for more information on a card"

- **Many of a kind** means one vertical list or tree, one line each: face, name, one state word, time. Not a grid of cards.
- **What's working shows at the top level** without a click. Idle ones collapse onto one line ("+6 idle").
- **Groups are headings, not folders you have to open** (siso-shell's rule). Only the detail folds.
- **Cards** only when each item needs a picture or several facts at once (rule 7).

## Check

five or more objects drawn as cards need a written reason. Live items are visible with no clicks.
