# ADR 0012: Decide; show only the real forks

Status: accepted, round 1 (7 Oct 2026)

> "STOP building variations. Think first principles"
>
> — Shaan, 8 Jun, model app · the UI board rule (15 Jun): "Don't ask Shaan to adjudicate individual cuts… where it is a genuine taste fork, show HTMLs and he picks"

- **Agents decide** everything that isn't a matter of taste or direction, and say what they decided.
- **A real taste fork** means two or three live versions side by side on one card, each with one line of reasoning and the agent's own pick.
- **Report with a URL** and a screenshot of what's on it, never a wall of text.

## Check

every message to you carries a URL. A review card with more than three options, or with no recommendation, goes back.
